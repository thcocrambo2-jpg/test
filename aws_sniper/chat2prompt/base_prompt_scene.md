**integrated_visual_description:**

Write a comprehensive, naturally flowing description divided into clearly labelled sections in the following order.

**1. Overall scene and subject:**

Begin by establishing the complete scene: the type of photograph, the subject, what she is doing, the location and the approximate framing. Describe her specifically: age (the brief's, or an apparent adult age category when it gives none), ethnicity, skin tone and undertone, general build and body proportions.

Describe her expression in detail: the position of her lips, whether they are closed or parted, whether teeth are visible, the direction of her gaze, the position of her eyebrows and the overall emotion conveyed. Explain the impression of the moment, such as relaxed, candid, conversational, amused or quietly confident.

Describe her facial characteristics as far as the brief sets them; otherwise keep them natural and ordinary. Include natural skin details such as pores, subtle texture, tonal variations, minor imperfections and the makeup level.

**2. Hair and facial details:**

Describe the hair's natural colour, thickness, density, texture, length, parting, hairstyle and how it is arranged. Explain where the hair falls, whether it is tied back, whether it forms a ponytail or bun, and its position relative to the head, ears and neck.

Include flyaways, loose strands, baby hairs and natural variations in texture. Describe the styling without making it unnecessarily elaborate.

**3. Clothing and accessories:**

Describe every garment in considerable detail. Include precise colours, fabric type, surface texture, neckline, shoulder straps, sleeves, stitching, seams, panels, waist construction, gathers, pleats, fit and natural fabric wrinkles.

Explain how the clothing sits on the subject's body. Describe how the garment follows the bust, waist, abdomen and hips, using natural and proportionate language. Include body proportions where they help explain the garment's fit.

Describe dupattas, sarees, scarves, bags and other accessories, including which shoulder they rest on, how they hang, where their straps run and how they overlap the clothing.

Include jewellery, earrings, necklaces, bangles, bindis and other adornments only when the brief or a reference's noted part calls for them, specifying their colour, material, approximate size and placement.

**4. Pose and body positioning:**

This section is particularly important. Set out the subject's complete physical positioning precisely enough to restage it.

Describe whether she is standing, sitting, leaning or walking. Specify the approximate angle of her torso relative to the camera and explain the orientation of her shoulders, chest, waist and hips.

Describe which shoulder is closer to the camera and which is farther away. Explain how her upper body is rotated, whether her waist follows the same rotation, and whether her body weight is shifted onto one leg.

Describe the position of each arm independently. Specify which arm hangs down, which is bent, where the elbow points, whether the hand rests on her waist, hip or behind her back, and which parts of the arms or hands are visible or hidden.

Describe her head position separately from her torso. Explain the direction in which her face turns, any slight head tilt, chin position and gaze direction.

Include the position of her shoulders, neck and visible legs. Keep the posture natural and anatomically coherent.

Use explicit viewer-left and viewer-right references wherever they prevent ambiguity.

**5. Camera angle and composition:**

Describe how the photograph is captured, including whether the camera is at eye level, chest level, slightly above or below the subject.

Explain the camera's horizontal position relative to the subject, whether it is directly in front or slightly to one side, and the perspective this creates.

Describe the approximate distance from the subject, the framing (head-and-shoulders, waist-up, medium portrait, upper-thigh-up, full body etc.), the subject's position within the frame and how much of the surroundings is visible.

Specify which parts of the body are included and where the frame crops them. When the whole head is in frame, say so explicitly with the space above the hair ("her whole head and face fully inside the frame, head not cropped"); never write vague placement such as "head near the top of the frame". Explain the relative size of the subject in the composition and how much of the frame she occupies.

Mention perspective distortion only when the brief's camera calls for it.

**6. Background and surroundings:**

Describe the location and its environment in detail: the brief's place, or the referenced background when a note asks for it.

Explain the arrangement of objects behind and around the subject, including shelves, cabinets, furniture, merchandise, wall decorations, ceiling features, windows, doors and other elements.

Include their approximate positions relative to the subject, colours, materials and appearance. Describe the depth and arrangement of the environment rather than simply listing objects.

Say whether the surroundings are busy, organised, cluttered or minimal, and which background elements stay recognisable and which are blurred.

Keep it a real, specific place; never a generic or aesthetically improved setting.

**7. Lighting and photographic appearance:**

Name the real light sources and their direction, intensity, softness and colour temperature: overhead fixtures, windows, ceiling lights, phone flash or other identifiable sources. Never write just "ambient lighting" or "natural lighting".

Describe how the light falls across the face, neck, shoulders, arms and clothing. Include the location and softness of shadows, highlights and natural variations in illumination.

Explain how the subject's lighting compares with the background. Describe the overall colour balance and whether the photograph has warm, neutral or cool tones.

Describe the depth of field, focus, background separation, smartphone processing, sharpening, compression and other photographic characteristics where they apply.

Keep skin natural and matte, with real texture. Do not introduce artificial glamour lighting, gloss, sweat or shine, dramatic cinematic lighting or beauty retouching unless the brief asks for them.

**photography_specs:**
