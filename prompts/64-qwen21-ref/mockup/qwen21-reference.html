New field
  • Numbers. The prompt names pictures by number, and the number is the slot the model reads (image_1…image_10). So each tile shows it as text, always, and it only changes when you reorder or remove.
  • Main. Image 1 is the main subject, and “Same as reference 1” takes its shape. It gets an accent frame and the word Main, so colour isn't the only signal. To change it, drag another image to the front.
  • On each tile (hover or keyboard focus): × removes, ⠿ drags to reorder, the chip shows the size. On touch screens × and ⠿ stay visible. Alt+← / Alt+→ moves the focused tile.
  • Full size. Clicking a tile opens it in the existing gallery Lightbox, big enough to check a face.
  • Add tile. Choose several files at once, drop several, or paste. It counts “3 / 10” and disappears at 10.
  • Recent generations is the strip every image input has; here a click adds the picture as the next number.
State 3 At 10 the Add tile goes, and at five columns the grid is two full rows (the template's 5 × 2). A drop that would pass 10 adds what fits and names what it left out.
State 4 While files are over it the whole field is the target, and the new tiles show the numbers they'll get before you let go.
State 5 Numbers update while dragging, so what you see when you let go is what the model gets. The prompt rewrite under Prompt is the optional part.
State 7 Refusals are shown in the field, next to what they're about, rather than as a toast; the rest of the drop still goes in.
